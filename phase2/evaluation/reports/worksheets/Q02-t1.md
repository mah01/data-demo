## Final answer

Revenue rose 2.6m DKK (+2.3%) in January–August 2026 versus the same months of 2025, but operating profit fell 5.4m DKK (−34%), from 16.0m to 10.6m. Operating margin dropped from 14.2% to 9.2%. H1 alone shows the same pattern (−4.0m DKK, 14.0% → 9.0%).

Three things did it: we sold less high-margin Service and more low-margin Equipment, Equipment discounts went up, and the German sales team grew without bringing in more revenue.

## 1. Less Service, more Equipment
**What the data shows (ERP, CRM):**
- **Margins by category:** Service earns about 59% gross margin, Equipment about 25%.
- **Service fell** 4.4m DKK (−14%). Almost all of the fall is in Denmark (−4.3m, −23%), where service agreements, technician hours and call-out fees each dropped about 24.5%. The Danish decline starts in Q1 2026.
- **Equipment grew** 7.7m DKK (+14%). Most of that is Denmark (+8.2m).
- **Danish service renewals** (CRM): the renewal rate fell from 83% (Sep 2024–Aug 2025) to 64% (Sep 2025–Aug 2026). Lost renewals went from 9 contracts worth 1.7m DKK to 18 worth 4.3m. Most were decided in December 2025, so the loss appears in 2026 invoicing.
- **Recorded reasons for the 18 losses:** customer brought maintenance in-house (7), price too high (4), lost to competitor (4), budget cuts (1), no reason given (2).
- **Same customers in both systems:** the customers who didn't renew (Køge Bygningsservice, Herning Foder, Fjord Energi and others) also have no service-agreement invoices in 2026 (ERP).

**Interpretation:** When a service contract ends, the customer also seems to stop buying technician hours and call-outs from us. Technician salaries sit in payroll, not in cost of sales, so they don't fall with the lost revenue. That makes the real profit hit bigger than the gross-profit figures show.

## 2. Equipment discounts went up
**What the data shows (ERP):**
- **Average Equipment discount** rose from 7.0% to 11.2%, and Equipment gross margin fell from 28.4% to 25.4%.
- **Not higher purchase costs:** per-unit costs match the standard cost list, so the lower margin comes from lower prices.
- **Germany:** discounts went up every quarter, from 6.7% (Q1 2025) to about 15.5–16% (Q2–Q3 2026). Equipment margin fell from 28.4% to 21.7%, and German Equipment gross profit fell 1.1m DKK even though revenue fell only 5%.
- **Denmark:** discounts jumped from about 7% to about 10.5–11% in Q1 2026. Margin fell from 28.4% to 25.6%.
- **Sweden:** stable at about 7% discount and 29.7% margin.
- **German deals (CRM):** won Equipment deals in 2026 carry 14–19.5% discounts. This applies to both long-standing and newly hired reps.
- **No recorded reason:** I found no CRM notes explaining a pricing policy or campaign. Why discounts went up is still unexplained.

## 3. Personnel costs (+2.35m DKK, +12%)
**What the data shows (payroll and ERP agree to the krone: 21.89m DKK):**
- **German sales team:** +1.77m DKK. Four hires between Sep 2025 and Jan 2026 took the team from 8 to 12 people.
- **Other departments:** ordinary pay rises of about 3–4%.
- **German results in the same period:** revenue fell 8%, the win rate on new deals dropped from 30% to 18% even with the deeper discounts, and "price too high" losses rose from 19 to 27 deals (CRM).

**Interpretation:** So far the bigger German team has cost more without bringing in more revenue. Four to eleven months may be too early to judge the new hires, but I see no sign of a payback yet.

## 4. Other operating costs (+0.64m DKK, +7%)
Travel (+0.21m), car costs (+0.21m), marketing and trade fairs (+0.20m) and rent (+0.16m) went up. Lower recruitment costs (−0.31m) partly offset this.

## Open questions worth asking
1. **Discount approvals:** who approved the higher discounts in Denmark and Germany, and was it a deliberate choice? The data doesn't say.
2. **Danish service contracts:** why did seven Danish customers bring maintenance in-house, and can any of the lost contracts be won back?
3. **German sales team:** what are the targets and the timeline for the expanded team to cover its cost?

Caveat: 23% of German lost deals have no loss reason recorded, so the CRM loss-reason shares are indicative only.
