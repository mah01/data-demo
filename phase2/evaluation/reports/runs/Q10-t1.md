## Final answer

I recommend management look first at Germany's sales performance, lost Danish service contracts, and discounting on Equipment. Together they explain most of the fall in operating profit, which dropped 5.4M DKK (−34%) from Jan–Aug 2025 to Jan–Aug 2026 while revenue grew 2.3%.

**Headline, from the finance system (Jan–Aug 2026 vs Jan–Aug 2025)**

| | 2025 | 2026 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Revenue | 113.2M | 115.7M | +2.3% |
| Gross margin | 40.0% | 37.1% | −3.0 pts |
| Gross profit | 45.3M | 42.9M | −2.4M |
| Personnel costs | 19.5M | 21.9M | +2.35M (+12%) |
| Operating profit | 16.0M | 10.6M | −5.4M (−34%) |

---

### 1. Germany: a bigger sales team is producing worse results
**Facts**
- **Hires:** four German sales staff were hired between Sep 2025 and Jan 2026. German sales payroll rose 1.77M DKK (+49%), which is about 75% of the company's personnel cost increase (payroll).
- **Revenue:** German revenue fell 2.3M DKK (−8.6%): Components −16%, Equipment −5%. German gross profit fell 1.8M (finance system).
- **Win rate:** new deals created in Germany rose 35% (+12.1M DKK), but the share of deals won fell from about 30–33% in 2025 to about 17% in Q2–Q3 2026 (CRM).
  - The four new hires won 0–20% of their deals.
  - Some experienced reps also won less, e.g. Stefan Brenner fell from 47% to 23%.
- **Accounts:** the new hires were largely handed existing customer accounts rather than new ones. The Germany country manager moved 13 companies from experienced reps to them in bulk.
- **Why deals are lost:** new loss reasons appeared in 2026, "Not qualified" (15 deals) and "No response" (11). For 23% of lost deals no reason was recorded.

**Interpretation (not proven):** the extra pipeline looks weaker, and moving existing accounts may have disrupted customer relationships. Combining lower gross profit and higher payroll, Germany accounts for about 3.6M DKK of the 5.4M operating-profit decline.

### 2. Denmark: lost service contracts
**Facts**
- **Revenue:** DK Service revenue fell 4.3M DKK (−22.6%) and gross profit fell 2.55M. This is the largest single driver of the gross-profit decline, and in the company's highest-margin category (about 59%).
- **Renewals:** in the Q4 2025 renewal round, only 59% of contracts were renewed (16 lost, worth 3.7M DKK a year), against 84% in Q4 2024 (7 lost, 1.2M) (CRM).
  - The most common reason was "Customer insourced maintenance": 7 contracts, worth 2.3M.
- **Cross-check:** the customers with the biggest ERP service declines are the same ones whose renewals were lost. Examples: Køge Bygningsservice −93%, Aabenraa Foder −91%, Vejle Foder −86%, Fjord Energi −100%.
- **Process:** all 16 lost renewals had one owner (Line Østergaard). The renewal deals were opened only 6–8 weeks before contracts expired.

**Why it's urgent:** no renewal deals exist yet for the 2027 contracts, which come up in Q4 2026 — only a few weeks away. There is still time to act. Whether customers doing their own maintenance is a lasting trend is not known from the data.

### 3. Discounts on Equipment have risen sharply
**Facts** (average discount on Equipment, from invoices)

| Market | 2025 | 2026 | Gross margin | Revenue | Gross profit |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Germany | 7.0% | 15.3% | 28.4% → 21.7% | −5% | −1.1M |
| Denmark | 6.9% | 10.8% | 28.4% → 25.6% | +28% | +1.25M |
| Sweden | ~7% | ~7% | slightly up | | |

- **Germany:** the discount crept up from mid-2025 and jumped in Feb 2026. CRM shows 14–20% discounts on nearly every German Equipment deal won, whether the rep is new or experienced. Yet the win rate kept falling and "Price too high" is still a top loss reason. The discounts are not winning deals.
- **Denmark:** the discount stepped up in Jan 2026, across all 23 Equipment products.
- **Sweden:** unchanged, so the rise is not company-wide.

**Rough size:** at 2025 discount levels and the same volumes, gross profit would have been about 3.0M DKK higher (≈1.3M Germany, ≈1.6M Denmark). That assumes volumes would have held, which is uncertain in Denmark. The CRM activity notes (searched for "discount" and "Rabatt") give no explanation; whether a pricing policy or approval limit changed around Jan 2026 needs to be asked.

**Other factors (smaller):**
- **Sales mix:** growth in low-margin Equipment and the drop in high-margin Service lowered the overall margin.
- **Operating costs:** other operating costs rose 0.64M (+6.6%), mainly travel +26%, marketing +23% and cars +19%.
- **Missing CRM data:** some loss reasons, markets and product categories are blank in the CRM, so the win-rate and loss-reason figures are indicative.
